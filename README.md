# cybersecurity-upskill
Cybersecurity & AI Governance Upskill
Welcome to my cybersecurity and governance repository! This space documents my hands-on upskilling journey through the Columbia University Cybersecurity Governance & AI Governance Program, featuring structured notes, applied projects, and interactive assessment tools.
🚀 Focus Areas
1. Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC): Framework implementation, risk assessment methodologies, and regulatory compliance standards.

2. AI Governance: Ethical AI deployment, risk mitigation, algorithmic accountability, and compliance in emerging technologies.

3. Security Operations & Policy: Bridging technical controls with organizational governance policies.

💡 Interactive Flashcard Engine
To test and reinforce core concepts from the cohort, this repository includes a Python-driven flashcard module. Topics cover framework structures, risk evaluation standards, and AI regulatory principles.
